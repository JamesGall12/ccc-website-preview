Our story

A small clinic with a big heart

We were founded in South Wales in 2021 by husband and wife Dr Dave Howells and Sam Ashton. We wanted it to be easier and kinder for people to get help from a specialist doctor, without feeling judged.

Today we are a small team in Barry, in South Wales. We see patients all over the UK by video. You get the same personal care from your first call onwards.

FAQs

Questions people ask us

Can't see your question? Ask us on the free chat.

Is medical cannabis legal in the UK?

Yes. Since November 2018, specialist doctors in the UK can prescribe medicines made from cannabis when they think it is right for a patient.

Will I definitely get a prescription?

No. The doctor decides after reading your records and talking to you. Sometimes another treatment is a better choice, and he will tell you if so.

Do I need a referral from my GP?

No. You can come straight to us. We do need a summary of your GP records, and we help you get it.

Do I have to travel to Barry?

No. You see the doctor by video, wherever you are in the UK.

How much does it cost?

The first chat is free. The first appointment with the doctor is £250. After that, the monthly plan is £33.33. Your medicine is paid for separately at the pharmacy.

What happens on the free chat?

A 10-minute phone call with our team. We answer your questions and explain the steps. No pressure, and nothing to pay.
